<p align="center"><b>Free accounting for everyone</b> – from private finances to freelancers, companies and non-profits.<br>
<sub>a <a href="https://falkoss.org">falkOSS</a> project</sub></p>

> spezado is at its beginning: there is nothing for daily use yet. Start with the
> [design](https://github.com/spezado/spezado/blob/main/docs/spec.md).

- **One person, many organisations** – one account for every set of books you keep or look after.
- **Local first** – an app, a terminal or a server; works offline, synchronised end-to-end encrypted.
- **Law as modules** – Germany first, then Austria, Switzerland and further European countries.
- **An open protocol** – data exchange follows [librotenado](https://github.com/librotenado), open for everyone.

### Same rules as falkOSS

- **Free licences** – AGPL-3.0-or-later; protocol library, companion apps and the API description under MIT; the
  brand kit's artwork under CC BY-ND 4.0; contributions with a DCO sign-off, no CLA.
- **Everything included** – one edition, every feature free; no paid tiers, nothing held back.
- **No telemetry** – nothing phones home.
- **In the open** – issues, plans, reviews and decisions happen in public.
